# Remédios de Ervas Portuguesas

## Product and purpose
Portuguese-language landing page for the user's informational product, exactly named Remédios de Ervas Portuguesas. The visitor discovers the product, previews the proposed contents and reaches the purchase section. Target audience: Portuguese-speaking adults interested in botanical traditions and learning about herbs. All brand details other than the product name are a creative proposal. Price, checkout URL, author credentials, format, exact contents and purchase conditions have not been supplied.

## Editorial direction
Primary library inspiration: Superdesign `nature-inspired-style`, adapted to the subject and the calm editorial direction below. Retain its organic photography and forest/cream contrast. Adapt its oversized industrial type and extreme rounding into the serif apothecary treatment specified here; this file is the final design specification.
Contemporary Portuguese apothecary meets refined botanical editorial. A welcoming, calm, beautifully composed page with generous negative space and large botanical still-life photography. Warm cream paper, forest green and restrained antique gold. Avoid generic SaaS layouts, heavy shadows, excessive pills, emoji icons and fake reviews. Portuguese azulejo detail may appear as one delicate line pattern, never a busy background.

## Design tokens
Background #F7F5EC. Alternate paper #EEEBDD. Deep green #183D31. Body ink #33463B. Muted text #667065. Gold #A17D45. White #FFFFFF. Borders #D9DDCE.
Typography: Cormorant Garamond for expressive display headings, italic accents and product title. DM Sans for body, buttons and navigation. Headings are 64–88px on desktop, 40–48px mobile; body 16–18px with line height 1.65. Small uppercase labels have 0.16em letter spacing.
Max content width 1200px. Section spacing 96px desktop, 64px mobile. Grids 24–40px gaps. Buttons deep green with cream text, 56px high, 4px radius, right-pointing arrow. Cards have restrained 6–12px radius and fine borders. Photography can use a large arch top or a rectangular editorial crop. No gradients.

1. Slim navigation: elegant product wordmark, O guia, Uma prévia, Perguntas frequentes, Conhecer o guia. Mobile uses accessible menu.
2. Split hero: left eyebrow SABERES DE PORTUGAL; title A tradição das ervas, ao seu alcance. Paragraph explicitly introduces an educational product and botanical heritage without health outcomes. CTA Conhecer o guia anchors to offer. Secondary Explorar a proposta anchors to contents. Right: richly textured herbs, chamomile, rosemary, mortar and tea on cream linen; tasteful floating digital-cover mockup labeled Remédios de Ervas Portuguesas and PROPOSTA DE CAPA. Small bottom metadata Tradição botânica / Conteúdo educativo / No seu ritmo, without unverified format or quantity.
3. Editorial introduction: Conhecimento que atravessa gerações. Brief evocative cultural copy without historical specifics or claims of proven effectiveness.
4. Suggested-content section clearly labeled PROPOSTA DE CONTEÚDO; heading Um novo olhar sobre as ervas. Three numbered editorial columns or rows: Conhecer as plantas; Entender a tradição; Aprender com responsabilidade. Wording must say estrutura sugerida, to be validated against the real product. No claims that the actual product contains unconfirmed modules or bonuses.
5. Interactive botanical preview: heading Comece pela curiosidade. Three selectable herbs Camomila, Alecrim, Hortelã with a matching photograph, evocative purely sensory descriptions and culturally neutral botanical curiosity; no indications, recipes, doses or medical advice. This is demonstrative content for the page, not quoted from the actual product. Clearly marked Prévia ilustrativa.
6. Dark forest green offer section: Seu próximo capítulo começa aqui. Product cover and exact name. Price area says Oferta em preparação, with no invented amount, discount or fake urgency. CTA Ver disponibilidade opens an accessible modal that candidly states the purchase link is being prepared. No form collection without a destination. Owner configuration instructions stay outside customer-facing UI.
7. Four accessible accordion FAQs: O que é este produto? (educational proposal; exact contents to be confirmed); É um produto físico? (format to be confirmed; do not promise delivery); O conteúdo substitui orientação profissional? (No, educational content); Como posso comprar? (offer and availability via CTA, no dead links).
8. Footer with exact product title, navigation and concise responsible-content statement: Conteúdo educativo. Não substitui orientação profissional. Do not add legal or privacy links to nonexistent pages.

## Content constraints
All copy in natural Portuguese. Do not fabricate efficacy, cures, disease treatment, contraindication advice, certifications, author experience, testimonials, customer counts, medical endorsement, refunds, guarantee, total pages, module quantities, access duration, instant delivery or an actual sale price. The product name is allowed exactly as supplied. Keep the product education-focused. Unknown business terms must remain unset. Distinguish illustrative preview and suggested contents clearly, aesthetically.

## Interaction, accessibility and responsive layout
Navigation anchors scroll to real sections. All visible CTAs do something coherent. FAQ uses native details/summary or correct ARIA. Herb selection is keyboard accessible. Modal closes on Escape, close button and overlay and restores focus. Mobile stacks hero text before image; navigation simplifies, all CTAs remain reachable, no horizontal overflow. Visible focus styles, sufficient contrast, semantic landmarks, alt text, touch-friendly controls. Motion only subtle 160–220ms fades; honor prefers-reduced-motion.
